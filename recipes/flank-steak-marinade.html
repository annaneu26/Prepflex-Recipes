Flank Steak Marinade

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1/4 tsp chili powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Flank steak

Instructions

  1. Combine the lemon juice, oil, ginger, chili powder, salt, pepper, soy sauce, and minced garlic.
  2. Marinate or baste the flank steak all day.
  3. Broil for 6–9 minutes on each side.
