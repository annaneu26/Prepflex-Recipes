Dad's Italian Beef

Slow-cooked Italian beef made with chuck roast, hot pepper rings and their juice, onions, beef broth, beer, garlic, and oregano.

Ingredients

  • 4–5 lb chuck roast
  • 1 can beef broth
  • 1 can beer
  • 1 16-oz jar Vlasic HOT pepper rings with juice
  • 1–2 onions, sliced
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Oregano, to taste
  • Water, only if necessary
  • Long Italian hard rolls, for serving
  • Extra onions, optional, for serving

Instructions

  1. Use a crockpot or a 13 × 9-inch baking dish covered with foil.
  2. Sprinkle both sides of the chuck roast with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and oregano.
  3. Lay the sliced onions and hot pepper rings over the meat.
  4. Pour in the beef broth, pepper-ring juice, and beer. Add water only if necessary. Cover.
  5. Bake at 300°F for 4 hours.
  6. When done, shred the meat with two forks.
  7. Serve on long Italian hard rolls, with onions if desired.
