Chicken Tetrazzini

Creamy chicken tetrazzini with mushrooms, white wine, Parmesan, crème fraîche, peas, and an optional panko topping.

Yield: 6–8 servings

Ingredients

  • 2–2½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts
  • 11.7 oz dry spaghetti
  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small yellow onion or 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups chicken stock, plus extra as needed
  • 1½ cups whole milk, plus extra as needed
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup finely grated Parmesan, divided, plus more to taste
  • ½ cup crème fraîche
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp dried thyme or 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Optional: ½–1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • Optional: ½ tsp MSG
  • Optional: ½–1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Optional topping: ½ cup panko + 2 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Season chicken with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 18–25 minutes until cooked through. Rest 10 minutes and cut into generous bite-size pieces.
  2. Brown mushrooms in 1 tbsp butter over medium-high heat for 7–10 minutes.
  3. Add 1 tbsp butter and onion or shallots; cook 3–4 minutes. Add garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.
  4. Add wine and reduce by about half.
  5. Add remaining 2 tbsp butter, stir in flour, and cook 1–2 minutes. Gradually whisk in stock, milk, and cream. Simmer 4–6 minutes until silky but still slightly loose.
  6. Lower heat. Stir in ½ cup Parmesan, crème fraîche, Dijon, nutmeg, and pepper. Adjust seasoning. For more depth, use optional MSG/Worcestershire; for brightness, add white wine vinegar a little at a time.
  7. Cook spaghetti about 2 minutes shy of al dente and drain.
  8. Combine pasta, sauce, chicken, and frozen peas. Add a splash of milk or stock if needed so it looks slightly too saucy.
  9. Transfer to baking dish. Top with remaining Parmesan and optional buttered panko. Bake at 375°F for 25–35 minutes. Broil briefly if desired.

Freezer notes

For individual freezer portions, freeze assembled but unbaked. Keep panko separate. If freezing ⅔ of the batch, loosen that portion with about ¼–⅓ cup extra milk or stock first. Bake from frozen at 375°F for 45–60 minutes covered, then 10–15 minutes uncovered; or thaw overnight and bake 25–35 minutes.
