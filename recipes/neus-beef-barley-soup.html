Neu's Beef Barley Soup

A family beef barley soup made with short ribs, tomatoes, green beans, corn, onion, barley, oregano, basil, and garlic.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef short ribs
  • 2 (28 oz) cans tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 pkg (10 oz) frozen cut green beans
  • 1 can (12 oz) corn, undrained
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup barley
  • 1/2+ tsp dried oregano leaves
  • 1/2+ tsp dried basil leaves
  • 1+ clove garlic, crushed
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 quart water

Instructions

  1. Place short ribs, tomatoes, salt, and bay leaves in an 8-quart kettle; add 1 quart water. Cover and bring to a boil. Skim the surface.
  2. Reduce heat; simmer, covered, for 2 hours.
  3. Add green beans, corn, onion, barley, oregano, basil, and garlic. Simmer, covered, for 1 hour longer.
  4. Remove the meat and bay leaves. Discard the bay leaves.
  5. Cool the meat. Cut into slices and add it back to the soup. Refrigerate for several hours.
  6. Just before serving, skim the fat from the surface. Slowly heat the soup to a boil.

Notes

The source recipe specifies refrigerating the soup for several hours before skimming the fat and reheating. Servings, prep time, and exact total time were not specified.
