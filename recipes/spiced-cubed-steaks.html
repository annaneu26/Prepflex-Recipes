Spiced Cubed Steaks

Cubed steaks marinated in soy sauce, onion, garlic, sugar, and ginger, pan-broiled in butter, and served on hot buttered French bread.

Ingredients

  • 8 large cubed steaks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • French bread

Instructions

  1. Mix the soy sauce, water, onion, garlic, sugar, and ginger.
  2. Pour the mixture over the cubed steaks and marinate them.
  3. Pan-broil the steaks in hot butter in a skillet for 1 to 2 minutes on each side.
  4. Serve on hot buttered French bread.
