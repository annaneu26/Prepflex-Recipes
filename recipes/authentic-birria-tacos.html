Authentic Birria Tacos

Rich pressure-cooked beef birria with guajillo and ancho chiles, served as crisp consomé-dipped tacos with Oaxaca cheese, cilantro, and onion.

Ingredients

Beef

  • 2–3 lbs chuck roast, cut into chunks
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Birria / Consomé

  • 3 dried guajillo chiles, deseeded
  • 3 dried ancho chiles, deseeded
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 plum tomatoes, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, whole
  • 5 cups water
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 cups chile water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cups beef broth

For assembling tacos

  • Corn tortillas
  • Oaxaca cheese, shredded
  • Cilantro
  • Onion

Instructions

  1. Sear the beef: Heavily season the chuck roast with salt and pepper. Heat a large pot over high heat and sear the beef in batches until each piece develops a crust. Remove the beef and set aside.
  2. Toast the chile mixture: In the same pot, add the guajillo chiles, ancho chiles, chopped onion, plum tomatoes, garlic, oregano, cumin, cloves, and black peppercorns. Cook briefly until lightly roasted and fragrant.
  3. Soften the chiles: Pour the 5 cups water over the ingredients. Bring to a boil and cook for about 10 minutes, or until the chiles are soft.
  4. Blend the sauce: Using a slotted utensil, remove the solids from the pot. Blend them until smooth with 2 cups of the chile cooking water, including the spices, cloves, and peppercorns.
  5. Pressure cook the birria: Return the blended chile mixture to the pot. Add the seared beef, 4 cups beef broth, 2 bay leaves, and 1 cinnamon stick. Pressure cook for 45 minutes.
  6. Shred the beef: Remove the cooked beef chunks and shred the meat.
  7. Assemble the tacos: Dip corn tortillas into the birria consomé. Fry them in a pan over medium heat. Fill with shredded Oaxaca cheese, shredded birria beef, cilantro, and onion.
  8. Crisp and serve: Cook the tacos until crisp on both sides. Serve hot with the birria consomé for dipping.

Notes

The source recipe does not specify servings, prep time, total time, or quantities for the tortillas, Oaxaca cheese, cilantro, or onion, so those values are intentionally left unspecified.
